About

I'm a PhD candidate in Computer Vision and AI at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, UNSW Sydney, developing AI methods for analysing medical imagery — with a current focus on colorectal disease diagnostics.

Before UNSW, I researched Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) applications in Sri Lanka, completing an MPhil on detecting leaf disease in persimmon via OCT imaging, alongside work on MRI-based brain tumor analysis. My broader interest is building AI systems that make complex medical data faster and more reliable to interpret, in support of better diagnostics and patient outcomes.

Research

AI-Driven Analysis of Colorectal Diseases Using Computer Vision and Machine Learning

Developing AI methods to improve the accuracy, efficiency, and interpretability of medical image analysis in colorectal disease diagnostics, at CSE UNSW Sydney under Prof. Arcot Sowmya, Dr. Sonit Singh, and Dr. Praveen Ravindran.

Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) Applications

Applied OCT and deep learning to early, non-invasive detection of circular leaf spot disease in persimmon (MPhil thesis), at the Faculty of Engineering, University of Sri Jayewardenepura under Dr. Udaya Wijenayake and Dr. Ruchire Eranga Wijesinghe.

MRI-Based Brain Tumor Analysis Using Machine Learning

Classified brain tumors as malignant or benign using demographic and texture features extracted from diffusion-weighted (ADC) MRI, at the University of Peradeniya.
